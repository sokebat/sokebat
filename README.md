## About Me

Fullstack developer based in Pokhara, Nepal, finishing a BSc in Computer Science & IT at Tribhuvan University (2022-2026). I build end-to-end products, pixel-accurate UIs down to the database schema, with React, Next.js, NestJS, TypeScript, and PostgreSQL.

Over the past two years I've worked as a Frontend Developer at Nep Tech Pal and Thoplo Technologies, and I currently take on freelance full-stack contracts, shipping features from design handoff through deployment. I care about clean architecture, database performance, and interfaces people actually enjoy using.
